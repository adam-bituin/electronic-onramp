How a project runs

The same three stages apply whether we are installing accounting software or cabling a new office.

  1. Step 1

    Assessment

    We meet by phone or on site, review your current systems and agree what needs to change. You receive a written scope and price.

  2. Step 2

    Implementation

    We install, configure and migrate your data, scheduling work outside business hours where downtime would affect you. Everything is tested before handover.

  3. Step 3

    Handover and support

    Your team is trained and you receive all passwords, licences and documentation. Ongoing support is available under an annual maintenance contract.

About Electronic On-Ramp

Electronic On-Ramp W.L.L. is an IT and software company registered in the Kingdom of Bahrain. We are an authorized Sage partner and an Intuit QuickBooks Certified ProAdvisor, and we implement and support business software for companies across the GCC, including ZATCA e-invoicing for businesses operating in Saudi Arabia.

Our engineers also install and maintain office networks, cabling, CCTV, Microsoft 365 and cloud systems, and protect them with firewalls and Acronis backup. Clients work with one team for their software and their infrastructure.

Frequently asked questions

How is pricing calculated?

Each project is priced on its scope. Complete the quote form and we will reply with an estimate, followed by a written proposal. We are also happy to meet first to discuss your requirements.

Which accounting software do you support?

QuickBooks Desktop, QuickBooks Online, Sage 50, Sage 200 Evolution, Sage Business Cloud Accounting and Sage Pastel Payroll & HR, with MyQuickCloud for hosting.

Can you set up ZATCA e-invoicing?

Yes. We configure e-invoicing that meets ZATCA requirements for businesses operating in Saudi Arabia and connect it to your accounting system.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Our clients range from offices of a few people to companies with several sites.

What is an annual maintenance contract?

A fixed yearly fee that covers preventive maintenance, support visits and agreed response times.

How quickly do you respond to support requests?

Response times are set out in your support contract. Issues that stop your business from operating are handled first.

Who owns the accounts and licences?

Your company. All accounts, passwords, licences and documentation are registered in your name and handed over to you.
